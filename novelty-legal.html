The Service is intended for users 18 and older; age is verified at signup. If you or someone you know shows signs of a gambling problem, the South African Responsible Gambling Foundation helpline (0800 006 008, responsiblegambling.co.za) offers free, confidential support.

7. On Licensing

NOVELTY is structured to be an information/prediction/subscription platform, not a bookmaker: it analyses fixtures, generates predictions, displays odds and implied probabilities, and ranks selections for a monthly subscription fee — it does not accept wagers, collect stakes, determine or pay gambling winnings, maintain betting accounts, or act as anyone's bookmaker. The National Gambling Board's own position is that online betting is legal when conducted through a provincially-licensed bookmaker; NOVELTY isn't conducting betting at all under that framing. This position is researched but has not yet been confirmed by a formal written legal opinion from a South African gambling-law specialist.

Separately from the licensing question, the NGB has issued specific guidance on gambling-advertising compliance under the National Gambling Act and provincial legislation. NOVELTY's marketing describes what it actually sells — analysis, predictions, statistical picks — and avoids language that reads as encouraging wagering (e.g. avoiding phrases like "place your bet" or "value bets" in favour of "statistical picks"/"predictions").
