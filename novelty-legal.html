Betting vs. Gambling — Definitions

Gambling is the broad legal category: staking money or something of value on an uncertain outcome for a chance to win more. It covers many forms, including casino games, lotteries, and sports betting.

Sports betting is one specific, well-defined form of gambling: staking money on the outcome of a sporting event. NOVELTY operates specifically in the sports-betting category — it is a sports-betting analytics and prediction service, powered by the NOVELTY AI-Engine. We use these terms plainly and accurately throughout this Service and these Terms, rather than euphemisms.
