About Us

Who We Are

Locust Harvest™

A technology and innovation company building practical apps for everyday life. We identify real-world challenges and explore how technology can turn them into useful digital solutions, opportunities and growth. Our philosophy is simple: every challenge can become an opportunity—from plague to profit.

What We Stand For

Turning Everyday Challenges Into Digital Platforms That Work

We started Locust Harvest™ with a simple belief: real edge comes from going deep on one problem, not spreading ourselves thin across a dozen ideas.

Practical First

Every platform we build is designed around a real, everyday problem — not a demo, not a trend. If it doesn't genuinely make life simpler for the people using it, we don't ship it.

Built to Scale, Built for the World

Our apps solve real problems across the globe. Our aim is to grow beyond our home market and build Locust Harvest™ into an international company.

Trust & Simplicity

Simple technology, honestly delivered. We'd rather earn trust slowly than promise more than we can build.

What We're Building

Premium App Suite™

All apps in this suite are premium quality. Paid access. No free options. Just useful tools for everyday use.

CALCULUS™

Everything You Want to Calculate

CALCULUS is your everyday calculator hub for quick, accurate answers — basic arithmetic, scientific functions, unit conversions, percentages, and everyday math made simple. Whenever you need a number crunched right the first time, CALCULUS has it covered. Premium plans with full access.

PATHFINDER™

Your Career Journey Starts Here

PATHFINDER helps you find jobs, build professional CVs, generate application letters, and discover career paths through our Career Path Finder quiz. Browse vacancies, learnerships and internships worldwide, with deadline alerts. All career tools — premium plans with full access.

WEALTHWISE™

Smart Money, Smart Future

WEALTHWISE is your personal finance toolkit — track income and expenses, calculate salary-after-tax, plan your monthly budget, check rent affordability, manage debt, set savings goals, and link your bank accounts via Plaid. Take control of your money, premium plans with full access.

SCHOLASTIC™

Study Smarter, Not Harder

SCHOLASTIC is the ultimate student study hub. Generate automatic study timetables, track exam countdowns, plan assignments, calculate grades, and get daily study reminders. Perfect for university and high school students juggling a busy term. Premium plans with full access.

ESTIMETA™

Know Before You Spend

ESTIMETA estimates home repair costs across 12 trades — plumbers, electricians, painters, roofers and more — and calculates fuel costs for any trip, from point A to point B, including cost per kilometre. Plan your expenses with confidence. Premium plans with full access.

FAMTREE™

Build Your Family History Before It's Forgotten

FAMTREE lets families build interactive trees, upload photos, record stories, and preserve memories for generations to come. Add a family timeline, invite relatives to contribute, and export your tree. Private, emotional, purpose-driven genealogy — premium plans with full access.
