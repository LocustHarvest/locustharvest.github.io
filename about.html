About Us

Who We Are

A Technology & Innovation Company

Locust Harvest (Pty) Ltd is a technology and innovation company building practical digital platforms for real, everyday problems — finding a trusted service provider, getting short-term professional cover, landing the right job, building better money habits, or booking a trusted beauty professional. Every platform is simple to use and built to solve one problem well.

What We Stand For

Turning Everyday Challenges Into Platforms That Work

We started Locust Harvest with a simple belief: the biggest opportunities are hiding inside the problems people deal with every day — finding a trusted service provider, getting short-term professional cover, landing the right job, building better money habits, or booking a trusted beauty professional. Instead of chasing trends, we build focused tools that solve one problem well.

That thinking runs through everything we make — from LOHAPLACE's services marketplace and LOCUPLACE's staffing network, to MYPLACER's job centre, WealthWise's financial-wellness game, CHOOSA's beauty subscription, and NOVELTY's sports analysis — all under the Plague2Profit philosophy of turning everyday challenges into ventures that work.

Practical First

Every platform we build is designed around a real, everyday problem — not a demo, not a trend. If it doesn't genuinely make life simpler for the people using it, we don't ship it.

Built to Scale, Built for the World

We build for real, everyday problems — the kind people deal with everywhere, not just in one place. Every platform is designed to grow beyond its first market as we build Locust Harvest into an international company.

Trust & Simplicity

Simple technology, honestly delivered. We'd rather earn trust slowly than promise more than we can build.
