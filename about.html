About Us

Who We Are

An African Technology & Innovation Company

Locust Harvest (Pty) Ltd is an African technology and innovation company developing practical digital platforms and applications for businesses, professionals and communities.

We build digital platforms for real-world industries. Our applications are designed to connect people, businesses, services and opportunities through simple, intelligent technology — not hype, not complexity for its own sake.

What We Stand For

Turning Everyday Challenges Into Platforms That Work

We started Locust Harvest with a simple belief: the biggest opportunities are hiding inside the problems people deal with every day — finding a trusted service provider, getting short-term professional cover, or making sense of a hard situation. Rather than chase trends, we build focused tools that solve one problem well.

That thinking runs through everything we make — from LOHAPLACE's marketplace, to LOCUPLACE's staffing network, to the Plague2Profit philosophy of turning setbacks into ventures.

Practical First

We build for real use cases and real industries, not for demos. If it doesn't solve an actual problem, we don't ship it.

Built for Africa, Built to Scale

Rooted in South Africa, engineered to grow — our platforms are designed to work for local conditions first and expand from there.

Trust & Simplicity

Simple technology, honestly delivered. We'd rather earn trust slowly than promise more than we can build.

What We're Building

Our Platforms

LOHAPLACE™ — a digital marketplace connecting customers with trusted service providers, businesses, products and opportunities.

LOCUPLACE™ — a locum staffing marketplace connecting nurses and nurse aides with healthcare facilities that need qualified, short-term cover. In design now, following the same model as LOHAPLACE.

Plague2Profit — our philosophy, media and social ecosystem: turning challenges into opportunities, ideas into solutions, and problems into productive ventures.
